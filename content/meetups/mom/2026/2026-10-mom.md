## Sessions

### AgentWeave: Exploring Multi-Agent Reasoning with Python

Speaker: Kayalvizhi

Discussion Summary

- Explored concepts around multi-agent reasoning and collaborative AI systems.
- Highlighted that assigning distinct roles to agents does not necessarily lead to diverse reasoning outcomes over time.
- Discussed conversation orchestration, state management, communication mechanisms, and agent interactions.
- Covered the importance of maintaining agent profiles, tracking conversation progress, evaluating interactions, and managing agent lifecycle events.

### Simulate a NPN Bipolar Junction Transistor in Python

Speaker: Rengaraj

Discussion Summary

- Presented a simulation-based approach to understanding transistor behaviour.
- Discussed the generic working principles of bipolar junction transistors and how simulations can aid learning and experimentation.
- Simulation provides a practical way to understand complex engineering concepts.
- Software-based experimentation can complement theoretical understanding.

### LLM Optimizations for Local Inference

Speaker: Vijay Kumar B

Discussion Summary

- Discussed challenges associated with using cloud-based AI models for customer-sensitive data.
- Explored the growing importance of local AI infrastructure and high-performance AI workstations.
- Reviewed optimisation techniques that improve AI performance, responsiveness, and resource utilisation.
- Covered foundational concepts behind how modern language models understand context and generate responses.

## Lightning talks

### AngelScript - Embedded Scripting in C++

Speaker: Praveen

Discussion Summary

- Presented the evolution of the developer workflow before and after introducing embedded scripting capabilities.
- Discussed the flexibility and productivity improvements that scripting can provide within applications.
- Also shared an experimental use case from personal experience involving thermoelectric cooling technology for ligament and tissue recovery.

### AI Model Collaboration and Accuracy

Speaker: Madav

Discussion Summary

- Discussed practical limitations of smaller language models (e.g. 7B), including accuracy and context retention challenges.
- Highlighted the inherently probabilistic nature of AI systems and the resulting variability in outputs.
- Explored collaborative AI approaches where different models perform complementary functions.
- Presented “Mixture of Agents” concepts for improving solution quality through multiple perspectives.

### Call for Experimentation using Jev (SystemOne)

Speaker: Sakthi

Discussion Summary

- Encouraged teams to actively experiment with emerging AI technologies and approaches.
- Introduced the SystemOne model and discussed potential application areas.
- Called for experimentation using Jev for a SystemOne based request processing.
